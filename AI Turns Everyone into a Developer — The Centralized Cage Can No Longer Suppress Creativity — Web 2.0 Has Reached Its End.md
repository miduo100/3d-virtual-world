The virtual world space (the metaverse) once heavily backed by top-tier capital and tech giants gradually lost its heat and was met with public indifference — and it was never because 3D virtual technology wasn't mature enough.

- **On the surface**: VR/XR hardware hasn't reached ubiquity, 3D content production costs are high, capital has pulled back
- **At a deeper level**: Platforms placed "controlling traffic" above "empowering individuals"

The core root cause is that the public tangibly felt the inherent constraints and monopolistic nature of centralized platforms. Giants armed with top-tier technology, elite talent, and massive capital remained fixated on controlling traffic flow, monopolizing ecosystems, and harvesting creator value to the extreme — all while ignoring that when individuals also possess the tools, that entire logic collapses.

> They forgot the internet's most fundamental principle: **Water can carry the boat, and water can also overturn it.**

The internet's territory and value should rightfully belong to every creator, every ordinary person.
